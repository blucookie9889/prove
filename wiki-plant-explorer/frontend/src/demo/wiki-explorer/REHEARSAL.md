- **Story:** hover a detail of the taro leaf, its line draws to "Legge di Murray", click; the thumbnail grows into the Lena delta while the doors fade to placeholders; the new doors arrive one at a time. Then the delta's channels lead back to the leaf through "Nervatura fogliare", so the loop ends where it began with a seen cause (about 15 s).
- **Poster:** the leaf with the Murray detail hovered and its line drawn.
- **Compact (below 640 px):** the same app on a phone, tapped; the connector is left out (it would cross the doors' text) and hooks are one line.
- **Filmed:** `film.mjs --gate` passes at 1280 and 390 wide. Left: `Swap`/`Fly` handoff notes (the kit's own crossfade and flight) and sampling gaps while the GPU was busy.
